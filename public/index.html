      About this prototype

      Terms

      Manual for anything is an experimental hackathon demo that turns photos and notes into generated instructions.

      Using the demo

      Upload photos you have permission to use. Review the generated steps before following them: the system can misidentify objects, miss details, and make mistakes.

      Use your judgment

      Check materials, measurements, tools, and equipment instructions yourself. Generated manuals are not a substitute for professional advice or equipment safety instructions.

      These are prototype usage notes. Formal service terms have not yet been published.

      About your uploads

      Privacy

      Your photo and optional notes are sent to this server to create a manual. Generation uses configured AI services to interpret the photo and produce instructions and artwork.

      Saved results

      The prototype saves uploads, generated results, and diagnostic files. Hosted deployments store files in Microsoft Azure Storage. On a hosted app, saved films are private to the account that created them. A film link requires signing in to that account. Local development uses a shared local account for this installation.

      Before uploading

      Avoid photos or notes containing personal or sensitive information.

      How long we keep your data

      We keep your photos, notes and generated films until you ask us to delete them, and no later than 90 days after the alpha ends. Message @josendev on X and we'll delete them within 30 days. Backups may keep copies for up to 35 more days.

      This prototype has no self-service deletion control. A full privacy policy has not yet been published.
