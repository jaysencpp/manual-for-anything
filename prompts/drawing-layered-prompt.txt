Style — an IKEA manual in colour: flat fills in the object's OWN colours as the photo shows them, at full strength (a red brick is red, a lemon is yellow, a white mug is white) — one flat colour per face, a lighter shade of it for lit faces and a darker one for shadowed faces; outlines in "#1d2b4f", round joins; a few interior lines for edges, seams and labels; no texture, no gradients, no photographic detail.
